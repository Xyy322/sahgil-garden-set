Design a modern, clean, and visually appealing landing page for a small business called Sahgil Garden Set, which offers garden furniture and landscaping services. Style & Mood: Bright, fresh, and natural color palette (greens, browns, and neutral tones). Friendly but professional vibe, showing trustworthiness and quality. Use clean typography and plenty of white space. Sections & Layout: Hero Section: Large background image of a beautiful garden or furniture set. Headline: “Transform Your Garden into a Dream Space.” Subheadline: “Quality furniture and landscaping services for every home.” Call-to-action buttons: “Get Started” and “View Products.” About / Features Section: Three cards highlighting main offerings: Garden Furniture, Landscaping, Custom Designs. Short descriptive text under each icon or image. Customer Benefits Section: Highlight reliability, quality materials, fast service. Use small icons or images for visual appeal. Testimonials / Reviews Section: Two or three short quotes from happy customers with small avatar images. CTA Section: Strong call to action: “Book Your Free Consultation Today.” Button linking to a signup or contact page. Footer: Links: Home, About, Services, Contact, Login/Register. Social media icons (Facebook, Instagram). Small copyright text. Additional Notes: Make the page responsive, simple, and easy to scroll. Include buttons and hover effects for interactive feel. Focus on clear hierarchy: Hero → Features → Testimonials → CTA → Footer.
